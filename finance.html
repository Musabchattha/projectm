Price Breakdown

What's Included in Our CIF Price?

✅ Included in CIF Price

🚗

Vehicle Purchase Price

The auction or dealer price of the vehicle in Japan, including auction fees and de-registration.

🚢

Japan to Mombasa Shipping

RORO or container shipping cost from Japan (Nagoya/Osaka port) to Mombasa, Kenya.

🛡️

Marine Insurance

Full cargo insurance covering the vehicle from Japan port to Mombasa. Required for CIF pricing.

❌ Not Included in CIF (Paid Separately)

💰

Uganda Import Duty (25%)

Paid to Uganda Revenue Authority. Based on CIF value. Use our calculator above for an estimate.

💰

VAT 18% + Excise Duty

18% VAT on (CIF + Import Duty). Excise duty of 10% for vehicles over 2000cc engine capacity.

📋

Clearing & Logistics Fees

Mombasa port handling, NipponAuto clearing service fee, SGR transit to Kampala, Uganda registration.
