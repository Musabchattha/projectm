Your Options

Finance Solutions

Choose the financing structure that best suits your lifestyle and budget.

🏦

Standard Loan

A straightforward fixed-term loan. Borrow the full vehicle cost (less deposit), make equal monthly payments, and own the vehicle outright at the end.

Pros
  • Clear repayment schedule
  • Own the vehicle outright
  • Fixed monthly payments
  • No restrictions on usage
Cons
  • Higher monthly payments
  • Interest adds to total cost
Apply Now
Most Popular
🎈

Balloon Payment

Pay lower monthly instalments during the loan term, with a larger "balloon" lump sum due at the end. Ideal if you expect a large cash inflow later.

Pros
  • Lower monthly payments
  • More cash flow flexibility
  • Option to refinance balloon
  • Keep or return vehicle at end
Cons
  • Large payment at end
  • Higher total interest cost
Apply Now
🔄

Lease-to-Own

A structured lease where your payments gradually build equity. At the end of the lease period, you have the option to purchase the vehicle at a predetermined price.

Pros
  • Lowest entry cost
  • Tax benefits (business use)
  • Flexible end-of-term options
  • Easier approval criteria
Cons
  • Usage/km restrictions
  • You don't own until final payment

Am I Eligible?

Finance Requirements

Basic eligibility criteria for our finance programs. We work with buyers in over 50 countries.

🪪

Valid Identification

Government-issued photo ID (passport, national ID or driver's licence) and proof of address dated within 3 months.

💰

Proof of Income

Last 3 months bank statements or payslips. Self-employed applicants require 12 months of financial records.

🏦

Bank Account

An active bank account in your name for deposit payment and regular instalment collection.

📋

30% Deposit

A minimum 30% deposit of the vehicle price is required to reserve and begin the finance process.

✅

Credit Check

We conduct a soft credit check initially. Full credit assessment may be required for larger loan amounts.

📝

Signed Agreement

A signed finance agreement and vehicle purchase agreement before funds are released and shipping begins.
