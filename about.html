Our Story

Kampala's Most Trusted
Japanese Car Importer

Founded in 2012 in Kampala, NipponAuto Uganda was born from a simple belief: Ugandan buyers deserve access to genuine, high-quality Japanese vehicles — at transparent UGX prices with zero hassle on import clearing.

What began as a small operation at Nakawa Industrial Road has grown into Uganda's most recognized Japanese vehicle importer. We've helped over 2,000 Ugandan families, businesses, and government agencies import their ideal vehicles directly from Japan's top auction houses.

Every vehicle we import is Grade 4+ certified, pre-inspected in Japan, shipped CIF Kampala, and cleared through Uganda Revenue Authority on your behalf. Our team of Uganda-based specialists handles every step of the import process — so you simply show up and drive away.

Our Journey

Company Milestones

From a small Nakawa yard to Uganda's most trusted Japanese car importer — here's how we grew.

2012

Founded in Kampala

David Kiggundu established NipponAuto Uganda at Plot 45, Nakawa Industrial Road with a team of four and a mission to bring genuine Japanese cars to Uganda at honest prices. First year: 38 vehicles imported.

2014

500th Vehicle Imported

Celebrated our 500th imported vehicle — a 2014 Toyota Land Cruiser Prado delivered to a Kampala business owner. Word-of-mouth referrals grew our monthly import volume to 15+ cars.

2016

URA Certified Clearing Agent

Became a Uganda Revenue Authority certified clearing agent, enabling us to handle all customs paperwork in-house and dramatically reduce clearing times for our customers.

2018

Launched Online Inventory Platform

Launched Uganda's first real-time Japanese car import inventory website, allowing Ugandan buyers to browse cars in UGX, request quotes online, and track their import status.

2020

1,000+ Vehicles Milestone

Reached our 1,000th imported vehicle despite Covid-19 challenges. Pivoted to full digital consultations via WhatsApp — a model that became our standard and further improved customer satisfaction.

2022

Hybrid & EV Division

Launched a dedicated hybrid and EV import division responding to rising fuel costs in Uganda. Now Uganda's leading importer of Toyota hybrid and Mitsubishi PHEV vehicles.

2024

2,000+ Vehicles & Showroom Expansion

Surpassed 2,000 total vehicles imported to Uganda. Expanded our Nakawa showroom to a 3-acre facility with 50+ cars on display and a dedicated customer consultation centre.
