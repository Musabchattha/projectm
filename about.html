Our Story

Built on Trust,
Driven by Passion

Founded in 2009 in Tokyo, Japan, AutoElite Motors began as a small family operation with a single mission: make the world's finest Japanese vehicles accessible to buyers everywhere.

What started as a team of three automotive enthusiasts has grown into a globally recognized export dealership, shipping over 10,000 premium vehicles to more than 50 countries. We maintain deep relationships with Japan's top auction houses, private sellers, and fleet operators to give our customers first access to the best stock.

Every vehicle in our inventory undergoes a rigorous 150-point inspection before export. We handle all documentation, shipping logistics, and aftersales support — so you can focus on enjoying the drive.

Our Journey

Company Milestones

From a small Tokyo office to a global export powerhouse — here's how we got here.

2009

Founded in Tokyo

Kenji Tanaka establishes AutoElite Motors with a team of three and a single motto: "Quality without compromise." First year: 48 vehicles exported to 3 countries.

2012

Expansion into Africa

Opened our first regional office in Nairobi, Kenya, enabling us to serve the booming East African market. Reached 500 total exports.

2015

Launch of Online Platform

Launched our industry-leading online inventory system, allowing customers to browse, inspect reports, and purchase vehicles entirely online — a first in the Japanese export market.

2018

Middle East Hub Opens

Established our Dubai office to better serve the Gulf Cooperation Council countries. Became the #1 rated Japanese export dealer in the UAE within 12 months.

2021

10,000th Vehicle Milestone

Celebrated exporting our 10,000th vehicle — a 2021 Toyota Land Cruiser delivered to a customer in Lagos, Nigeria. Over 50 countries served.

2023

EV & Hybrid Division Launched

Launched our dedicated electric and hybrid vehicle export division, responding to growing global demand for sustainable transport options.

2024

New Yokohama Inspection Center

Opened a state-of-the-art 5,000 m² inspection and preparation facility at Yokohama Port — the largest Japanese export vehicle inspection center in operation.
