Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

How long does shipping take?

Shipping times vary by destination. Africa (East & West) typically 4–6 weeks; Middle East 2–3 weeks; Pacific Islands 4–8 weeks. We provide real-time tracking for all shipments.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. All vehicles come with a 12-month AutoElite powertrain warranty. Extended warranty packages of up to 36 months are available at additional cost.

Can I inspect the vehicle before purchase?

Absolutely. We provide detailed inspection reports, 360° photos, and video walkthroughs. You're also welcome to arrange a third-party inspection at our Tokyo facility.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept international bank transfer (T/T), letter of credit, and select finance arrangements. A 30% deposit is required to reserve a vehicle, with the balance due before shipping.

Do you handle customs clearance?

We prepare all necessary export documents (deregistration, export certificate, BL, etc.). We can also connect you with trusted customs clearing agents at your destination port.

Can you source a vehicle not in your inventory?

Yes! We have access to Japan's major auction houses and a vast dealer network. Share your specifications and budget and we'll source your perfect vehicle, often within 1–2 weeks.
