Simple Import Process

How to Buy From NipponAuto Uganda

We handle the entire import process for you. Here's how it works step by step.

01
🔍

Choose Your Car

Browse our online stock in UGX or visit our Nakawa showroom. Tell us your budget and preferred model — we can source anything from Japan.

02
💰

Pay Deposit

Pay 30% deposit to secure your car (bank transfer, mobile money, or cash at our office). Balance due before collection.

03
🚢

We Import & Clear

We ship from Japan, clear customs at Mombasa, pay URA duties on your behalf, and transit the vehicle via SGR to Kampala.

04
🎉

Collect & Drive

Pay remaining balance and collect your car from our Nakawa yard. We sort Uganda plates, logbook, and roadworthiness certificate.

Uganda import costs typically include: 25% Import Duty on CIF value, 18% VAT, 10% Excise Duty (for vehicles over 2000cc), 6% Withholding Tax, plus clearing and agent fees. Use our Finance page to calculate your total cost.
