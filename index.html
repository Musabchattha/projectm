Simple 4-Step Process

How to Buy From NipponAuto Uganda

From browsing in Kampala to driving your Japanese car — we handle every step of the import process.

01
🔍

Choose Your Car

Browse our online stock or visit our Nakawa showroom. Tell us your budget in UGX and preferred model. We'll show you the best options.

02
💰

Pay Deposit

Pay a 30% deposit to secure your vehicle. We import directly from Japan's top auction houses, ensuring Grade 4+ condition cars only.

03
🚢

We Import & Clear

We handle shipping from Japan, customs clearing at Mombasa port, Uganda Revenue Authority (URA) duties, and transit to Kampala.

04
🎉

Collect & Drive

Collect your car from our Nakawa yard in Kampala. We handle Uganda number plates, logbook, and road worthiness certificate for you.
