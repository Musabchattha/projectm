📝 Vehicle Description

This exceptional 2022 Toyota Land Cruiser Prado TX is one of the finest examples of Japan's most popular 4WD for Uganda's roads. Sourced directly from a top-tier Japanese auction house and achieving a Grade 4.5 rating — near perfect condition inside and out.

The Prado TX is widely regarded as the go-to SUV for Uganda, delivering exceptional performance on Kampala roads and upcountry terrain alike. With full-time 4WD, Terrain Select, and a powerful 2.8L diesel engine, this vehicle handles everything Uganda's roads can throw at it.
