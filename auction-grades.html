Japanese Auction Grades Explained

Everything you need to know about Japanese car auction grades — so you can buy with confidence from NipponAuto Uganda.

🇯🇵

What Are Japanese Auction Grades?

Japan has the world's most transparent and well-regulated used car auction system. Every vehicle that passes through a Japanese auction house is independently inspected and assigned a grade by a certified appraiser. The grade reflects the overall condition of the vehicle — covering both exterior (body panels, paint) and interior (cabin, upholstery, equipment).

These grades are internationally recognised and are the gold standard for importing used Japanese cars. NipponAuto Uganda exclusively sources Grade 4 and above vehicles to ensure you receive a high-quality, near-perfect condition car.

Quality Standards

Auction Grade Reference Table

Grades are assigned by independent certified appraisers at Japan's major auction houses.

5 Excellent / Near New No defects whatsoever. Paintwork is perfect. No scratches, dents, or repairs. Perfect. No stains, tears, or odours. All electronics function perfectly. Essentially as good as a new car. Extremely rare and commands a premium price. Often low mileage demonstrators or late-owner trade-ins.
4.5 Very Good Plus Very minor marks only — tiny light scratches barely visible. No dents or repairs. Excellent. Minimal wear consistent with careful use. All functions work. Outstanding quality. Very hard to distinguish from Grade 5 without close inspection. NipponAuto preferred grade.
4 Very Good Very clean overall. May have 1–2 very minor scratches or small marks, but no dents or rust. Good condition. Slight signs of use but no significant wear. All equipment functional. Excellent value. Most NipponAuto imports are Grade 4. A Grade 4 car looks and drives like new. Our minimum import standard.
3.5 Good Plus A few minor scratches or small dents. May have had minor bodywork repairs. Fairly clean with some signs of regular use. Minor wear on seats or trim. Generally good condition but with visible minor cosmetic issues. Not imported by NipponAuto — we set a higher standard.
3 Good Multiple minor scratches, small dents, or possible rust spots. Body repairs evident. Signs of use — some staining, wear on seats or carpets. Equipment mostly works. Average used car condition. Suitable for budget buyers who plan cosmetic restoration. Not sourced by NipponAuto.

Previously written off or involved in a serious accident. Avoid unless you have expert knowledge. NipponAuto never imports R-grade vehicles.
Reading Auction Sheets

Common Auction Sheet Marks

Japanese auction sheets use standardised marks to indicate specific types of damage or wear on the vehicle's body diagram.

A

Scratch

A surface scratch on the paintwork. Small "A" = minor. Large "A" = deep or long scratch.

U

Dent

A dent in the body panel. Small "U" = minor dent. Large "U" = significant dent requiring panel work.

W

Wave / Warp

A wavy or warped panel — often from a previous repair or impact. Indicates previous bodywork.

C

Crack

A crack in a plastic or glass component — e.g. bumper, headlight lens, or windscreen.

X

Needs Replacement

The component is damaged beyond repair and requires full replacement. Significant mark.

B

Stone Chip / Small Mark

Tiny paint chip typically caused by road stones. Usually minor and common on older vehicles.

S

Rust

Surface rust on body panels. Major rust ("S" mark on structural areas) is a serious concern to watch for.

P

Paint Repair

An area that has been repainted. May indicate previous accident repair or paint fade correction.

E

Exchange / Replaced Part

A panel or part that has been exchanged or replaced — could indicate accident repair.

XX

Large Replacement Needed

Major damage requiring full panel replacement. Found on R-grade or heavily damaged vehicles.

How to read numbers with marks: Auction sheets often pair letters with numbers (e.g. "A2" = 2 scratches, "U1" = 1 minor dent). The number indicates quantity or severity. NipponAuto reviews all auction sheets before bidding and rejects any vehicle with major marks or structural damage.
